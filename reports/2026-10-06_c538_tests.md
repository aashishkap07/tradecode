# Two other ways, tested beside your plan (C538)

6 Oct 2026. You asked for Round 19's two close calls, deciding every 8 hours and using a 3-day
average, to run **in parallel** with your once-a-day rule. You also wanted their results on the
dashboard in plain words, "at appropriate intervals".

## 1. What runs now

Your plan is unchanged. Beside it, two **paper copies** run. Each started as an exact copy of your
daily ledger: the same 10 pairs, the same prices, the same $996.30. Each changes **one thing**:

| copy | the one change | decides at |
|---|---|---|
| **Checks every 8 hours** | looks at the 7-day gap three times a day | 06:00, 14:00, 22:00 IST |
| **Uses a 3-day average** | judges each gap on its last 3 days instead of 7 | 06:00 IST |

Everything else is identical: the same coins (only Pi42's), fees, 18% GST, 10 pairs of $100, and
whole Delta contracts.

**Analogy:** three cooks, one recipe, the same shop and the same ingredients. One cook tastes the
pot every 8 hours instead of once a day. Another judges the taste on the last 3 days instead of
the last week. Whatever their dishes do differently comes from that one change and nothing else.

**What they share:**
- **The same prices.** At 06:00 IST both copies use your daily run's own prices and rent records,
  fetched once, so the comparison is exact and the exchanges are not asked three times.
- **No transfers.** The copies don't move money between Delta and Pi42. Moving money costs every
  ledger the same $1, so it is left out of the comparison.

## 2. When the results update

| when (IST) | what |
|---|---|
| **06:00, every day** | **The scoreboard.** Your rule and both copies are counted at the same moment, on the same prices. This is the only time all three are counted together, so it is the only fair moment to compare them. |
| 14:00 and 22:00 | The 8-hour copy decides again. The page shows what it did ("closed X; opened Y" or "no change"), not a score. |

**The page shows, under "Two other ways, tested on paper":**
- a small table: your rule, each copy, its result since 6 Oct, and **ahead / behind yours** in
  dollars;
- the date of the score and the number of days;
- for each copy: what it does, its last decision, how many of its pairs are the same as yours, and
  the last 7 days;
- **how to read it**, from history:
  - every 8 hours came out about **$2.90 a month ahead**, in 8 months out of 10;
  - the 3-day average about **$6.40 a month ahead**, but only in 6 months out of 10, with swings
    from −$2.70 to +$23.50 a month.
  - So a few days, or even a month, prove nothing.

**Elsewhere:**
- **The log:** one line per decision, in words. The 06:00 IST line carries the score, for example
  "since 6 Oct: this rule +$1.23, your daily rule +$1.10 (ahead by $0.13)".
- **The 8-minute status block:** a TESTS line, for example "every 8 h: ahead $0.13, 3 days".

## 4. What decides it

The copies don't change your plan.

**1 November monthly review.** It reports their scoreboard against history's expectation.

**2 December research refresh.** It re-runs the history with October and November added, at the
same bar, beside these copies' real results. A copy replaces your rule only if:
- it passes that bar on the longer history;
- **and** it is ahead on the new months, both in the replay and in its own record here.

Otherwise your rule stays and the copies keep running.
