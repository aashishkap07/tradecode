## 3. Checked and correct, with the reasoning

- **The momentum variants and the range volatility are the research code,
  number for number.** This is proven on every day and coin in
  `omega_c510_test.py`. Today's live plan matches its own tournament row
  exactly.
- **The carry ledger and intraday shadow started at $249.94, not $250.**
  That's by design. Both are "duplicate paper accounts" that begin at the
  account's equity when they first run, and they first ran a minute after
  the book had paid its $0.06 of fees. The spot pot and the tournament have
  their own fixed $250.
- **"1d" on the carry and shadow panels after 16 minutes** counts days that
  have a record (one so far), not elapsed time. The spot pot's "0d" is the
  age of its positions.

## 4. Design points to know (not bugs)

1. **Quantity steps at $250.**
   - **What happened:** NEAR is held at $9.54 against a $7.60 target
     (NEAR trades in whole coins of $4.77). UNI is held at $8.96 against
     $6.59 (1 UNI = $8.96).
   - **Why:** each target rounds to the nearest step Bitget allows. This is
     the known small-account lumpiness (Atlas #18), and it shrinks as equity
     grows.
2. **The tournament's first day.**
   - **What happens:** it scores each rule close to close, as the research
     does, so tomorrow it credits every rule with the whole of 29 Sep. The
     book only started at 08:44 UTC.
   - **What to expect:** on day 1 the traded row and the real book will
     differ by the 00:00→08:44 move. From day 2 they should differ only by
     execution (steps, the $6 minimum, the band, bid/ask).
3. **The book is long TRUMP; the carry ledger is short TRUMP's perpetual.**
   - **Why the book is long:** its carry sleeve ranks coins once a week, on
     Monday's 7-day funding. TRUMP's was slightly negative, so the crowd was
     short, and N2 also removed its momentum short.
   - **Why the carry ledger is short:** it uses today's rate, and TRUMP's
     funding has since turned positive (15.5%/yr).
   - **Why it's fine:** different clocks, both paper, and no money conflict.
     The weekly clock is what the research tested.
4. **The carry ledger buys coins at Bitget's default funding.** APT, UNI,
   WLD and XLM show 11%/yr, which is the default 0.01%/8h = 10.95%/yr, just
   above its 10%/yr entry bar. That's real, if thin, income, which the 0.40%
   round-trip cost repays in about 2 weeks. The entry bar is up for review
   in December (Atlas #10).
