## 2. The shadow's −7%: real, and a coin-selection defect

**It is real.** I re-ran the shadow's own code on Binance's real hourly
candles for the same 26 hours. The replay reached $455.74 at 06:00 UTC; the
server showed $464.42. Same direction, same size.

**Why it lost:** the shadow chose its coins by **24-hour volume, with no age
filter**. On Binance that pulls in new meme listings. For example, 龙虾
(listed 11 Mar 2026) fell **32% in one hour** on $85M of volume. That is a
real Binance candle, not a data error.

**This was not the research's rule.** The research that tested the intraday
model chose coins by **30-day median volume** and only coins at least
**90 days** old (the same rule as the book, `_c488_universe`). The live
shadow drifted from it. That is a specification defect, and C522 fixes it.

**Honest caveat:** the research rule would also have lost about 10% over
these 26 hours. 龙虾 is old enough to pass the age filter, and the hourly
P&L swung with a standard deviation of 0.56%, against the research's ~0.11%.
The intraday model weights every coin equally and does not scale by
volatility, so a meme coin moving 30% in an hour dominates it. **This is
why it stays paper only.**

**The book is protected.** It sizes each coin by that coin's own volatility,
and 龙虾 is not in its top 20.

## 3. What C522 changes

1. **The shadow uses the research's coin rule** (30-day median volume, age ≥
   90 days, from the book's own saved inputs). The old record is not deleted:
   it is archived with its dates, return and trade count, and is shown on the
   panel ("archived …"). The new record starts at the next hour from the account's equity then (about $499).
2. **Max drawdown counts from the start.** The tournament showed "max DD
   0.0%" for a rule that had only fallen since day one, because the peak was
   taken from the first day's close, not from the starting $500. Fixed.
3. **Delta vs the Binance book on the same window.** The panel compared
   Delta's 5 minutes of life with the Binance book's whole month. It now
   takes one snapshot at Delta's first rebalance and compares both from that
   moment: "since 02 Oct 07:21: Delta x% vs the Binance book y%". It also says
   how old Delta's prices are.
4. **Delta names what it does not hold.** Targets under the book's $6 floor
   (today: WLD, FIL, PUMP, ADA, ENA) were skipped silently. They are now
   listed as "not held (under one contract or the $6 floor)", the same way
   the Binance book names its own.

Tests: `omega_c522_test.py` 25 checks: the universe rule and both fallbacks,
the archive (once), max DD, the Delta snapshot, the $6 floor, and the page in
Chromium. Full battery: 43 of 44 pass. The exit test needs a data folder (`corpusL/`) that this copy does not have, as before.
