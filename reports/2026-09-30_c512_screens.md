**Why the positions show +$0.43 in total but "book open" says +$0.36.**
- The per-coin figures are price gain only.
- "Book open", and so marked equity, also subtracts the fee you would pay to
  close everything: 0.06% × $113.90 = $0.07.
- So marked equity is what the account would hold if you closed out now. The
  fee is not counted twice: the $249.93 realised figure already paid the
  *entry* fees; this is the *exit* fee.
