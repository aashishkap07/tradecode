## 3. The maths and the principles, in one place

**Everything is measured relative to something, never in fixed dollars.**

| part | the rule | relative to what |
|---|---|---|
| a coin's size | ∝ 1 / its own volatility (from each day's open, high, low and close) | its own turbulence |
| the three habits | each scaled by 1 / its own 60-day volatility | each other |
| the whole book | leverage = 26.7% ÷ the book's own 60-day volatility, at most 3× | itself |
| trend | the average sign of 7, 14, 28 and 56-day returns | its own past |
| momentum | 14-day return minus beta × the market's; no short where funding is already negative | the market |
| carry | short the coins whose 7-day funding is highest, long the lowest | the other coins |
| trading | only when a position is >30% away from its target | its own target |
| the month guard | close everything if (month anchor − marked) ≥ 20% of the anchor | this month's start |
| cross-venue | s = 7-day mean of (Delta − Binance) funding × 365; in at \|s\| ≥ 20%/yr, out < 10% or a sign flip | the other venue |
| Savings | idle = marked − (margin + 20% + 5% of marked); at Binance's live rate | margin in use, the dial |
| tax (C528) | tax = your slab × max(0, net profit) | your profit, never a loss |
| the odds | P(a 12-month average ≥ 2%/month), from 20,000 bootstrapped years | history |

**Seen adjusting itself in the year below:**
- The book's exposure ran from **0.40×** (Feb–Mar 2026, rough markets) to
  **1.33×** (Dec 2025, calm).
- The cross-venue trade swapped up to 18 pairs a month as spreads moved.

**What is fixed on purpose:** the rules' windows and thresholds. A rule
that re-fits itself to last week chases noise.

**One principle applied wrongly (found now, not yet changed):**
- **What's wrong:** your plan's book runs on Delta, but its carry habit
  ranks coins by **Binance's** funding. On Delta it pays **Delta's**
  funding, which agrees with Binance's only 0.44 of the time.
- **What it costs:** that mismatch is most of the **3.7% a year** extra
  funding the book pays on Delta.
- **The proper fix:** rank by Delta's own funding when the book is on
  Delta. That's a rule change, so it needs a written test first (round 17).
  On today's numbers it can only help.

## 6. The 75% question

**Your target** is 2–4% a month with above 75% probability. Measured as
"averaging at least 2% a month over a year, after tax on net profit":

| | top slab 31.2% | 10.4% slab |
|---|---|---|
| planning case (book cut by ⅓, cross-venue stress-tested) | **81%** | 89% |
| pessimistic (the cross-venue edge halved again) | 51% | 65% |

- **The planning case clears 75%; the pessimistic case doesn't.**
- **The 28 Nov paper trial decides which is closer to the truth.** Passing
  it (≥ +3.7% over 8 weeks after costs, ≥ 6 of 8 weeks positive) is
  evidence for the planning case. That is why it is a gate before any
  money goes in.
- **"Every month" is a different question.** About 1 month in 6 still
  loses in the plan.
- **No rule can promise ≥ 2% in each single month** at these odds. The
  only thing that pays a steady amount every month is a deposit, at
  0.55–0.67%.

## 8. What changed in the code (C529)

1. **The Delta book's own exit:** a month guard on its own marked equity
   (closes everything at the dial, nothing new until next month), and it
   closes when the main book halts. Saved, loaded and shown on its panel.
2. **The cross-venue trade's two accounts:**
   - each venue's side is tracked (it always sums to the ledger's P&L:
     tested through real ledger runs);
   - `margins()` gives each side's equity and leverage;
   - `margin_watch()` warns once a day at 65% and gives the reserve line at
     50% (`C529_XV_WARN_AT`, `C529_XV_RESERVE_AT`);
   - the panel shows both sides.
3. **The screens:**
   - the $2,000+ sum is removed;
   - YOUR PLAN and the cross-venue ledger appear in "What is running";
   - "this run" uses marked equity (like the log) and "today realised" is
     labelled;
   - the cross-venue and carry panels show the funding settled since their
     run (per pair);
   - the log's PLAN row replaces TOTAL;
   - the boot banner and the Savings line are worded truthfully.
4. **Tests:** `omega_c529_test.py` (29 checks), plus updates to the C524,
   C527 and C486 tests. Research: `research/c529_year_walkthrough.py/.txt/.json`.
