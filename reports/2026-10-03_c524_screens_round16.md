## 2. Logical discrepancies found, fixed in C525

1. **The cross-venue ledger lost the midnight settlement (a C524 bug).**
   - It booked each pair's funding for settlements *after* the last
     cut-off, then moved the cut-off to midnight UTC.
   - So from the second day on, the 00:00 UTC settlement fell outside every
     window. That is 1 of 6 settlements on a 4-hour coin and 1 of 3 on an
     8-hour coin.
   - It has not cost anything yet: the first day it would bite is 4 Oct.
   - **Fixed:** the window now starts *at* the last cut-off. A new test runs
     a third day and checks all six settlements. Under the old code it fails
     (0.2585 vs 0.3135).
2. **The cross-venue ledger asked Binance for 100 funding records per coin.**
   An hourly coin has 192 in 8 days. Now 1000.
3. **The cross-venue panel showed "prices +$0.00, funding +$0.00".** That is
   correct for a ledger marked once a day, but it looked like a fault. The
   panel now says it is marked, funded and traded once a day, at 00:30 UTC.
4. **"rule tournament (9 rules)" above, 7 rows below.**
   - The two round-14 rules hold no book until the next rebalance, and the
     panel dropped them silently.
   - It now names them: "not scored yet (they join at the next rebalance)".

## 4. Binance vs Hyperliquid funding (H1): passes, but too thin to use

The same rule that found the Delta trade, unchanged, with Hyperliquid in
Delta's place: 173 coins listed on both, 2024-10 → 2026-09.

| | net per year | t | months positive | worst month |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **as pre-registered** | **+8.1%** | 3.7 | 21 / 24 | −2.3% |
| the midnight payment credited conservatively | +7.9% | 3.3 | 21/24 | −2.8% |
| same asset on both | +8.1% | 3.7 | 21/24 | −2.3% |
| **costs ×5** | **−23.9%** | −8.2 | 2/24 | −4.4% |
| costs ×10 | −63.8% | −15.2 | 0/24 | −8.3% |

- **It passes the bar I set**, but it earns about 0.65% a month at best.
- **It dies if trading costs are 5× my assumption.** For these small coins,
  real spreads are often that wide.
- The gaps fade fast: a coin's 7-day gap predicts its next 7 days at +0.40,
  against +0.71 on Delta.
- **Why so different from Delta:** anyone in the world can arbitrage
  Hyperliquid against Binance, and they do. Delta India is reachable only
  by Indians, which is why its gap survives.

**My recommendation: not added as a paper ledger.** By the rule I wrote it
qualifies. But a paper ledger replays the same cost assumption it is most
sensitive to, so it could not tell us what we need to know. It would also
need an on-chain wallet and more API load, for at most 0.65% a month. If
you want it anyway, it's a small addition: say so.

**The lesson for the project:** the Delta trade is the robust one (it passes
even at 10× costs) because of India-only access. The same mechanism on a
global venue is mostly arbitraged away.
