## 2. One thing still wrong, fixed in C498: "Session −$4.18"

Twenty minutes after the restart, the log said
`SESSION $-4.18 -1.66% peak $252.22 dd 1.66%`, and the web chart showed a
drop from $252.22. **The book had moved only a few cents in those 20
minutes.** The $4.18 was the book's open loss *from before the restart*.

The session starts from **realised** equity ($252.22) but is compared with
**marked** equity ($248.04), so every restart booked the book's open P&L as a
loss (or gain) of the new run. This is the same kind of mix-up C495 fixed for
the month guard.

**C498:**
- The session now runs **marked to marked**. It starts from the book's value at
  its first price after the restart. The SESSION row, the 8-minute "Session:"
  line and the chart all agree.
- The chart no longer starts with a realised point.
- The "Session:" figure now also includes the book's **realised** profit and
  loss when a rebalance closes a position. Before, it only counted intraday
  trades.

Your screen would have read about `SESSION $-0.4 -0.2%`: what this run actually
did.

**None of this touches trading.** The session loss breaker is off, the Guardian
only reports, and the emergency close only looks at intraday positions (there
are none). I checked all three in the code.
