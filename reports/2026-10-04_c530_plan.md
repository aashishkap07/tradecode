### The swinging PLAN line

**What I saw:** the plan's profit jumped by up to $4 between 8-minute rows.
The Delta book barely moved ($502.10 → $502.21). The cross-venue ledger's
daily mark was fixed at $499.94.

**The cause:** the two legs of each cross-venue pair were priced at
**different moments**:
- Delta's prices are read every 5 minutes;
- Binance's are read every 30 seconds.
- On 4 Oct, AIN moved **up to 5.6% within 5 minutes** (Delta's own
  1-minute candles, 07:00–08:00 UTC).
- One leg moved and its hedge seemed not to. At ~$110 a leg that is about
  ±$4.

This was **not real profit or loss.**

**The fix (C530):**
- When the bot reads Delta's prices, it also takes Binance's prices **at
  that same moment**.
- Both legs are valued from that pair of readings. A hedged pair now looks
  hedged.
- The test moves Binance 5% after Delta's reading: the plan's line does not
  move.

## 3. The AIN episode and round 17

**What happened:**
- On 2 Oct the cross-venue trade went short AIN on Delta and long AIN on
  Binance at $0.0242.
- AIN rose **+122%**, to $0.0536.
- The pair was flat: −$0.13 in total.
- But the **Delta account** fell to 60% of its half while the **Binance
  account** rose to ~130%.
- **Live, the Delta account could have been liquidated even though the
  trade made no loss.** That is the risk of running one trade on two
  separate accounts.

**Round 17**, written and pushed before testing
(`research/c530_preregistration.md`, results in
`research/c530_xvenue_sizing.txt`):
- **The question:** should each leg be sized by its coin's own volatility
  (X1v) instead of a flat 10%?
- **The test:** two years of real history, every assumption made harder
  (costs ×5, Delta's mark price, timing, liquidity).
- **Risk:** the worst month's weaker account improves from **28.9% to
  40.5%** of its half.
- **Return:** net falls from **+65.6% to +44.4% a year**, which is 68% of
  the original. The bar was 70%.
- **Verdict: not adopted.** The flat 10% stays, and the risk is handled by:
  - the **margin watch** (warn at 65%, reserve at 50%);
  - the **reserve**;
  - a **monthly rebalance** between the two accounts.
- **How often it matters:** in the history, a side fell below 50% in **12%
  of months** (about 1 in 8).

## 5. Pendle, honestly

**What it is:**
- A Pendle "PT" is a stablecoin's interest **sold in advance**.
- You buy it below $1, and on its date it pays exactly $1. The yield is
  fixed the day you buy.
- **The risk is not prices.** It is the stablecoin itself and the smart
  contracts.

**The rule** (written before building, `research/c530_preregistration.md`):
- **Universe:** stablecoins on Ethereum or Arbitrum, from an established
  issuer (Ethena, Sky, Liquity, Agora, Spark, Aave, Frax) or flagged
  "prime" by Pendle.
- **Filters:** $3M+ liquidity, 21–200 days to its date.
- **Choice:** the highest yield. It is held to its date, then rolled.

**On today's real list** (83 markets, 08:46 UTC):
- **Only two qualify:**
  - **Ethena sUSDe, 5.17%**, 53 days to 26 Nov ($4.2M liquidity);
  - Sky sUSDS, 5.05%.
- **Left out, by the rule:**
  - USDai (11%, but only 11 days left);
  - reUSD (12%) and sUSDx (19%), from unlisted issuers. A high yield
    there means high risk.

**What $100 earns, held to its date:**

| reading | cost per purchase | net a year | vs Savings 6.67% |
|---|---|---|---|
| as registered ($1 Ethereum gas, on purpose cautious) | $1.06 | **−2.35%** | worse |
| today's real gas (0.17 gwei, ETH $2,700: ~$0.20) | ~$0.26 | **≈ +3.2%** | worse |
| the same $100 left in Savings | — | **+6.67%** | — |

**Why it loses at $100:** the fixed cost of a transaction is a big slice of
a small amount. Every ~53 days it pays gas again, against 0.7% of interest
per period.

**The tax side:**
- Pendle is a VDA (30% + cess on the gain, a loss offsets nothing).
- Savings interest is taxed at your slab.

**My view:**
- Keep Pendle **on paper**: it is running, and the panel shows all of this
  live.
- **Don't fund it with real money at $100.**
- It makes sense on Arbitrum (gas in cents) once a qualifying market
  appears there, or at $1,000 or more.
- The panel compares it with Savings every day and turns amber when Savings
  pays more.

## 6. The odds after your change

**How this is measured:**
- **The book:** its whole 2020–26 history, cut by ⅓ (the overfitting
  haircut), less Delta's extra funding (3.72% a year).
- **The cross-venue trade:** the stress-tested history, now **in whole
  Delta contracts at its own size**.
- **The method:** 20,000 random 12-month paths.
- **Tax:** on net profit only. Pendle's gain is taxed apart at 31.2%.
- **The script:** `research/c530_plan_odds.py/.txt`.

**Whole contracts cost something at $250:**
- At $500, 20 of 182 wanted entries are skipped (one contract is bigger
  than the leg); at $250, **49 of 210**.
- The stress-tested return falls from **+69.6% to +59.0% a year**.

**P(averaging ≥ 2% a month over a year, after tax)**, planning case:

| plan | slab 0% | 10.4% | 20.8% | **31.2%** | bad year (1 in 10) | losing year |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **C530: $500 + $250 + Pendle $100** | 79% | **75%** | 69% | **61%** | +1.26%/month | 2% |
| $500 + $250 + that $100 in Savings | 80% | 76% | 70% | 63% | +1.34% | 2% |
| $500 + $250 only ($750) | 83% | 80% | 75% | 69% | +1.44% | 2% |
| **C528: $500 + $500** | 93% | **92%** | 89% | **85%** | +2.36% | 0% |

**Pessimistic case** (the cross-venue edge halved again): C530 **55% / 40%**
at the 10.4% / 31.2% slabs; C528 67% / 54%.

**What this means, as a trader would put it:**
- **The cross-venue trade is the strongest thing you have.** It is also
  almost uncorrelated with the book (−0.02). Halving it removes the most
  return per dollar of anything in the plan.
- **The cut doesn't make it safer in percentage terms.** AIN would have
  taken the Delta side to 60% at $250 or at $500. Size changes the
  dollars, not the percentages.
- **Your 75% target, under the new split, holds only at the 10.4% slab or
  below.** At the top slab it is 61%.
- **Under the C528 split it held at every slab** (85% at the top).
- **My recommendation:**
  - if the cut was about the AIN scare, put it back to $500
    (`C524_XVENUE_EQUITY = 500.0`) and keep the reserve at $200;
  - the margin watch and reserve are the protection, not a smaller size.
- **It's your money and your call.** The paper ledgers now run the split
  you asked for.
