## 3. What you are actually trading, in plain words

### Perpetual futures ("perps")
- A perp is a bet on a coin's price that never expires.
- **Long** = you win if the price rises.
- **Short** = you win if the price falls.
- You don't own the coin. You put down a deposit ("margin"), and the
  exchange settles your gains and losses every day.

### Funding: the "rent"
- A perp's price is kept close to the coin's real price by a payment every
  few hours, called **funding**.
- When more people want to be long, longs pay shorts. When more want to be
  short, shorts pay longs.
- Think of it as **rent**: one side is the tenant and pays, the other is the
  landlord and collects.
- Each exchange sets its own rent. For the same coin, the rent can differ
  between Delta and Pi42.

### The trade: collect the rent difference, cancel the price risk
- For one coin, say KAITO:
  - On Delta, longs pay shorts about 100% a year. **The bot goes short
    there**, so it is the landlord and collects.
  - On Pi42, the rent is near zero. **The bot goes long there**, the same
    size, and pays almost nothing.
- **If KAITO rises 10%:** the short on Delta loses $10, and the long on Pi42
  gains $10. The price change cancels.
- **What's left is the rent difference.** That is the profit.
- **Analogy:** you rent a flat in one building for ₹10,000 and sublet the
  same kind of flat in the next building for ₹12,000. You don't care whether
  property prices go up or down. You earn the ₹2,000 gap.
- **Rules:**
  - Open a coin only when the gap has averaged at least **20% a year** over
    the last 7 days.
  - Close it when the gap falls under **10% a year**, or when it flips
    direction.

### The amounts, exactly ($1,000 plan)

| | Delta Exchange India | Pi42 |
|---|---|---|

- **Each coin is a "pair":** about $100 short on one exchange and $100 long

### Why two accounts need "evening out"
- Each exchange holds its own half. Money can't flow between them by itself.
- **Example:** if KAITO rallies, the Delta short loses and the Pi42 long
  gains. Overall you are even, but the Delta account gets thinner.
- **Analogy:** two piggy banks for one household. One gets emptied by the
  bills and the other fills up. Every so often you move coins from the full
  one to the empty one.
- **The rules (C531):**
  - At the daily run, if one side is below **65%** of the two sides' average,
    the richer side sends it half the difference ($1 fee).
  - Once a month, it also evens them out if they differ by more than 2%.
  - Every hour the bot checks each side:
    - at **65%** the log warns;
    - at **50%** it says **"move $X from Pi42 to Delta NOW"**.
- In paper, the bot does this itself. When you go live, it will be **your
  bank transfer**: withdraw rupees from one exchange and deposit them on the
  other.
- **From history:**
  - about **11 transfers a year**;
  - one side dips below 50% in about 17% of months;
  - the lowest any side went was 48%.
- **The update's preview did exactly this.** Run on your server's own
  ledger with today's live prices, the Delta side was at 64%, and Pi42 sent
  it $179.48.
