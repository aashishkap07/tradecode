## 2. Why the bot restarted at 06:05 IST on 21 Oct

Your output shows:
- **no reboot:** the server has run continuously since 2 Oct, 12:19 IST;
- **not the watchdog:** it only said "no scan recorded yet, leaving it alone", from 06:35;
- **not a crash:** a crash shows "Main process exited" or "Failed". This was a clean
  `Stopped omega.service` → `Started omega.service` at **06:05:09**.

Something asked the system to restart the service. The likely cause is **Ubuntu's automatic security updates**:
- they run every day at **06:00 IST plus a random delay of up to an hour** (`apt-daily-upgrade.timer`);
- after updating a library, a helper (`needrestart`) restarts every service that uses it. Your server's clock is IST:
  the systemd lines and the bot's own log agree to the second.

**Why it matters:**
- Your plan's daily run is at **06:00 IST** and takes about 25 seconds, so a restart can land in the middle of it.
- On paper that is harmless: nothing is saved until the run finishes, and the run simply repeats after the restart.
- With real money, a restart while the two orders of a pair are being placed is exactly what B2 must guard against.

**The fix:** move the update window to **03:30-03:50 IST**, when nothing runs (the earliest daily job is 05:35). The
security updates still happen every day.

**Analogy:** the building's maintenance crew used to come at opening time. Now they come at 3:30 in the morning.

The commands are in section 5. The first block confirms the cause (read-only); the second moves the window. Moving it
is harmless even if the cause were something else.
