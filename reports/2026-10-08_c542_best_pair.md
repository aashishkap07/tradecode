## 6. The risk I found: one coin jumping several times over in a day

When I looked into why 20 pairs wiped out an account, it was real, not a glitch:
- **What happened:** on 15 Oct 2025 the coin BLESS rose **+530% in one day** on both exchanges.
  - The 20-pair runs happened to hold it, short on Delta.
  - That account lost about $520 in a day while the CoinDCX account gained the same.
- **Analogy:** a seesaw whose one end shoots up so high the other end goes through the floor. The two accounts
  together are fine, but one of them is empty before money can be moved across.

How often this happens, in CoinDCX's coin list over two years:

| a coin moving in one day | per 10 coins held, per year |
|---|---|
| ≥ 50% | about 6 times |
| ≥ 100% | about 1 time |
| ≥ 400% | about once in 10 years |

**What this means for each setting:**
- At **$100 a bet** (your plan), a +100% day costs one account $100, which the next daily run evens out. A +400% day
  would empty it.
- At **15 × $120**, the same events come about 1.8× as often and hit harder.
- **The 10- and 15-pair runs passed only because they didn't happen to hold BLESS that day.** The "lowest account"
  check catches this risk by luck, not by design.

**Your plan carries this risk today too.** One of your current pairs, **H**, rose more than 100% in a day four times
in the past year.

**Decision:**
- **10 × 10% stays.**
- **15 × 12% runs beside it as a paper copy** ("15 pairs of 12%").
- Before any bigger setting, the next research round tests protection against jumps:
  - each bet on its own margin, so a jump can only take that bet's margin, not the whole account;
  - the other half of the pair closed at once when that happens;
  - possibly leaving out coins with a history of jumps.
- That protection matters for real money in any case. It is now part of the order-path step (B2, 15 Nov).

**Lower fees through limit orders?** That adds only about +0.26% a month even if every order filled at the cheaper
"maker" fee. It's not a big lever.

## 8. Making a CoinDCX key (read-only, for the bot)

**Before you start:**
- Do this on CoinDCX's **website** (coindcx.com) on a computer or phone browser.
- **Never paste the key or the secret into chat or a screenshot.**

**Step 1: create the key**
1. Log in → your profile → **API Dashboard**.
2. **Create API key**, name it `omega-read`.
3. **Permissions:**
   - if it offers a choice, pick **read / view only**;
   - leave trading and withdrawals **off**.
4. **Bind IP address:**
   - if it lets you **type** an address, tick it and enter your server's: **140.245.30.104**;
   - if it only offers to bind to the device you're on (your phone or computer), **leave binding off**. A key bound
     to your phone would not work from the server.
5. Confirm with the OTPs (email and phone).
6. It shows the **key** and the **secret** once. Keep that screen open for step 2.

**Step 2: put it on the server, without showing it** (in Termius):

```
sudo -u omega bash /home/omega/omega/deploy/omega-keys.sh coindcx
```

Paste the key, press Enter. Paste the secret, press Enter. **Nothing appears on screen; that is normal.** It answers
only with the lengths.

**Step 3: check it (within 10 minutes):**

```
sudo journalctl -u omega --since "15 min ago" --no-pager | grep -E "C540" | cut -c1-300
```

**What you should see:**
- **Good:** `CoinDCX: futures … ; rupee wallet INR … , 0 position(s) … nothing is traded live`.
- **"CoinDCX refused the key":** a typo, or the key is bound to another IP address. Run step 2 again, or make a new
